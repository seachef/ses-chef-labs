# Synthetic public-contract fixtures

The three experiment-*-v3.json files are synthetic local test data generated by the reviewed local SQL contract tests. They are not live observations, private account exports, owner-session data, credentials, activation requests, or evidence of a trading strategy's performance.

- experiment-public-v3.json: simulated trial-one status and complete audit history.
- experiment-delayed-fx-v3.json: simulated complete audit history with delayed FX settlement.
- experiment-general-stop-v3.json: simulated general engine Stop with an older internally coherent experiment projection.

Only local test modules read these files. Production runtime modules do not load them. Keep this notice with the fixtures; never present their prices, fills, identities, balances or results as current activity.
