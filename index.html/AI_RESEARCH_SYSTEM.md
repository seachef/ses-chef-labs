# 🧠 AI Research System - How It Works

## 🎯 What This App Does

**Sea Chef Labs is an AI-powered crypto research system** that continuously analyzes 49 tokens and automatically selects the **TOP 3 picks** with the highest win potential based on:

### 🔬 Research Criteria:
1. **💧 High Liquidity** - Easy in/out trading without slippage
2. **🔥 Hype & Momentum** - Social sentiment and price momentum
3. **📊 Volume Analysis** - Trading volume across all exchanges
4. **⚡ Price Action** - Technical analysis and price patterns
5. **🎯 Win Probability** - Overall score combining all factors

---

## 🔄 How the AI Research Works

### Step 1: Continuous Analysis
- AI monitors **all 49 tokens** simultaneously
- Analyzes **61 data sources** (DEXes, CEXes, aggregators)
- Tracks **26 blockchain networks**
- Updates every **30 seconds** with live prices

### Step 2: Scoring System
Each token gets a **research score (0-100)** based on:
- **Liquidity Score** (0-100) - How easy to buy/sell
- **Volume Score** (0-100) - Trading activity
- **Momentum Score** (0-100) - Price movement
- **Hype Score** (0-100) - Social sentiment
- **Overall Score** (0-100) - Combined win probability

### Step 3: Auto-Selection
- **Top 3 tokens** with highest scores are displayed
- These are your **AI Research Picks**
- They have the **best chance of winning**

### Step 4: Continuous Rotation
When you **SELL** one of the top 3:
1. AI immediately promotes the **next best researched token**
2. A **new token starts researching** (progress bar appears)
3. The cycle continues - always showing the **best 3 picks**
