# Reviewed research and manual exchange handoff

The compact EXECUTION row now accepts controlled public research snapshots and can offer a qualified **Review Buy** link. The production feed is empty: no genuine current setup, source evidence or personal OKX fee evidence has been supplied. It therefore remains WAIT. Offline synthetic fixtures prove the qualifying path; they are excluded from the public build and are neither market observations nor fills.

**Review Sell** is separate. The owner selects the exchange and pair where they hold the asset and checks the session-only confirmation. This enables ordinary exchange navigation even when Buy research is missing, expired or unavailable. The app does not verify holdings, infer them from Buy clicks, store a balance, select an order side or place any order. Changing venue or pair clears the confirmation; reload clears all selection state. Final side, quantity, current price, fees and any protective orders must be checked and confirmed entirely on the exchange.

## Controlled input and review boundary

The public build contains two literal same-origin JSON inputs, `research-setups.json` and `research-reviews.json`. They must be published together in the same authorized repository/deployment update. There is no browser import, arbitrary URL, approval checkbox or automatic setup producer. Both files have schemaVersion 1 and the same nonempty revision ID. Their contracts are respectively `public-spot-research-v1` and `exact-research-review-v1`. Empty arrays are a valid WAIT state. At most eight unique setup IDs and venue/pair tuples are permitted; every setup requires exactly one matching review.

`reviewed-research.mjs` is the executable schema. Unknown fields (including private account fields), missing fields, accessors, sparse/annotated arrays, invalid values, duplicate IDs/markets/reviews, different revisions and unsupported versions fail closed. The loader reads only the two fixed HTTPS paths with GET, omitted credentials, no redirects, no cache, JSON media type, a 64 KiB cap per file and a five-second deadline. A partial, failed or canceled refresh clears prior approvals. No automatic polling or public exchange scanner is added. The existing Refresh and Cancel controls also operate on these two inputs.

A setup contains:

- `id`, `venue`, `pair`, `quoteCurrency`, reviewed `entry`, `stop`, increasing `targets`, and `expiresAt` (epoch milliseconds).
- `market`: matching venue/pair/currency, `product: spot`, exact allowlisted spot-page `sourceUrl`, source and receipt times, positive bid/ask and available ask quantity, and `captureSha256` of the source capture actually inspected during review. Entry must equal the observed ask; stop must be below entry and all targets above it.
- `fees`: the same identity and product, exact source URL, check/expiry times, evidence capture digest, fee basis, `orderType: market`, and both buy and sell basis points. CoinSpot requires its Markets source and 10 bps per side. OKX requires `okx-personal-pair-confirmed` and genuinely confirmed applicable personal pair fees. Unknown personal fees do not qualify.
- `costs`: `model: market-both-sides-adverse-v1`, a public hypothetical modeling quantity no larger than the observed ask depth, and adverse slippage basis points for each side. This quantity is not a private holding or an order instruction.

Each independent review record binds `setupId`, SHA-256 `setupDigest` of the entire normalized setup, `marketDigest` and `feeDigest` of the full respective evidence objects, reviewer ID, `decision: approved`, review time and expiry. Generate digests with the exported `researchDigest()` only after evidence extraction and review are complete. Canonicalization sorts object keys and preserves arrays and numeric values. The browser snapshots validated input before asynchronous hashing, recomputes all three digests, and rejects any mismatch. A changed target, source time, fee, size, identity or evidence digest requires a new reviewed setup digest.

The independent reviewer must inspect the actual source captures and verify the extracted facts and fee applicability before issuing the separately controlled approval. Source URLs and caller-provided capture-hash strings alone do not establish factual truth or reviewer authority. The browser validates the complete normalized evidence and its binding to the controlled manifest; it does **not** retrieve the underlying capture or independently authenticate an exchange account's fee tier. The trust boundary is authorized publication of an independently reviewed manifest. Keep private captures/account identifiers out of the public files and repository; publish only the minimal approved public research facts and non-secret evidence digests. If facts cannot be verified or safely reduced to that contract, keep the feed empty.

## Timing, costs and display

Research snapshots have a maximum five-minute age from the source timestamp and must expire no later than that ceiling, their fee evidence or review. This is a research freshness ceiling, not an executable/live quote guarantee. Review must follow source receipt and fee verification. Future clocks, expired data and clock rollback keep Buy at WAIT. Fee evidence has at most a one-day lifetime. A refresh can safely deliver an already-expired record, but it never enables Buy. Deployment latency may consume the entire snapshot lifetime; that correctly leaves WAIT.

Validation runs on load; freshness is checked on each display update, visibility return and Buy click/auxclick. Expiry removes the Buy URL. Pagehide cancels loaded research; the owner must refresh after returning. Sell confirmation remains independent of research freshness. Accepted research displays entry/stop/targets, modeled size, both fees, slippage, modeled buy cost and first-target/stop net outcomes, source/receipt/review/expiry times and source/review references. The model charges entry at observed ask plus adverse slippage and buy fees, and treats target/stop levels as hypothetical future bid prices minus adverse slippage and sell fees. The first target must be positive after those costs and the stop outcome negative. Neither depth, targets nor protective execution are guaranteed.

CoinSpot Markets fees are not interchangeable with its instant/conditional stop/target products. The static copy retains the supplied official fee research checked on 12 October 2026: Markets 0.1% per side, instant/conditional 1%. A reviewed model still needs its own current evidence. OKX's static personal fee remains unknown unless a genuine reviewed record supplies an applicable rate. No cheapest-venue claim is made. Existing Coinbase USD quotes cannot qualify an OKX or CoinSpot setup; its separate quote-age and paper acceptance gates are unchanged.

## Navigation and privacy

`exchange-destinations.mjs` contains ten exact frozen HTTPS spot destinations: OKX BTC/ETH/SOL against AUD/USDT, and CoinSpot BTC/ETH/SOL/USDT against AUD. Unlisted markets fail closed; there is no symbol interpolation. Buy, Sell and the generic Open link use the same selected spot page without side/quantity/query parameters, order prefill or app deep links. The generic link is always just navigation, even while Buy is WAIT. Click/auxclick handlers recheck exact venue, pair, displayed selection and destination. New tabs use `noopener noreferrer` and `no-referrer`.

Repeated clicks perform no economic action and create no account/fill state. There are no credentials, signing, wallets, order APIs, storage, account connections or holding payloads. The source loader receives public research only. The historical paper report remains unchanged and ended/stale. Page visits or exchange returns cannot update holdings, fill status, protection or that report. STOP and Cancel do not cancel exchange orders. This frontend change does not verify or modify the separate paper engine or start any worker.

## Verification and limits

Focused offline tests cover a full qualified loader-to-Buy handoff, evidence/cost tampering, invalid identity and source routes, missing/duplicate/expired records, time rollback, display/click expiry, input mutation during hashing, failed/canceled refreshes, exact URL safety, repeated clicks, independent Sell confirmation and venue/pair conflicts, privacy, production-empty inputs and the closed public asset list. Synthetic fixtures are labeled and not deployed. DOM and responsive CSS checks cover collapsed compact structure, explicit labels, 44 px controls, wrapping and narrow-screen single-column selectors. The mobile overview places the title and PUBLIC / READ ONLY / UTC labels in one wrapping header and reduces outer row spacing. It retains the existing text size, freshness/WAIT state, four tap-to-expand detail rows, exchange controls and always-visible ENGINE.

Native/mobile rendering and exchange app handoff remain unverified: this environment's Chromium sandbox helper is unusable, and no unsandboxed browser or sandbox modification is permitted. DOM/CSS tests do not establish rendered viewport behavior. No backend, paid service, persistent credential, continuous worker, demo order or real order is introduced.
